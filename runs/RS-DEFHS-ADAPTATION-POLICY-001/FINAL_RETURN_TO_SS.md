## 10. KILL RULE

Kill a valid completed adaptation for seed/fold instability, one-fold-driven
gain, class collapse, V-A contradiction, absent pair improvement, overfit/source
fitting, no meaningful parent difference, or absent system marginal value.
Incomplete/invalid/over-budget runs close technically and are distinguished
from a scientific rejection. No second parent/family/rank/LR/layer/augmentation
or full-tuning attempt follows a kill.

## 11. HAPPY-CLASS RULE

Happy receives the same pair scan and class-harm checks as every other class.
Its old weak F1 is not a trigger. A witnessed ranking deficit with unresolved
clean errors supports geometry; cheaper fixes, uncertain/common margins,
dependency concentration or inconsistent folds argue against adaptation.
Never relabel clips from model disagreement or apply happy-specific weighting.

## 12. ENSEMBLE-VALUE RULE

Actual held-out low-dimensional blend marginal value and ablation govern
membership. A weaker complementary frozen lane can outperform a redundant
stronger one as a system component. Adapted standalone superiority does not
override lost system value. Correlation and oracle union are diagnostics, not
membership scores. Equal weights first; coarse nonnegative weighting only;
leave-one-V-B-fold-out meta-validation; no unrestricted stacker.
